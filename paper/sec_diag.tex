\finding{Removing the lag separates artefact from exposure.} After seeing E7 and E9 we added diagnostic rows that repeat
them with the lag removed, comparing rod and palm velocities from the same step (Addendum A6, frozen before running).
In E7L the shielded failures disappeared: 0 of 2{,}560 shielded lifts failed, and the largest slip was 0.12\,mm. The E7
failures were an artefact of our emulator. In E9L they did not: with friction allowed to drop at 5\,mm/s, which the
6.5\,mm/s creep at the onset edge (Fig.~\ref{fig:anatomy}) exceeds without any lag, B4g, B4t and B5 still failed 364, 187 and 366 of 800 lifts.
