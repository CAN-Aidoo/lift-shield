\textbf{Probe failures (E2, E3).} With $\mu_s\in[0.2,0.4]$ the probe's starting grip, chosen for $\mu=0.3$, is already below
the breakaway grip for many objects; with 1.5\,mm monitor noise, the noise alone crosses the 2.5\,mm threshold within the
first 300\,ms hold. In both cases the probe returns its default $\Fs=30$\,N. The model controller then grips at 30\,N, the
residual's features fall far outside the training range, and the shield, whose bound is computed from $\Fs$, finds no
certified fast lift and declines. A probe that starts from a higher grip and treats a first-step motion as ``unknown'' rather
than as a measurement would avoid this; we did not change it.

\textbf{Bound-tightness sweep.} 2{,}160 lifts: grips of 85, 88, 91, 94, 97 and 100\% of the no-onset grip, lift times 0.37,
0.5 and 1\,s, $r\in\{1.0,1.1,1.2\}$, 40 objects, bound evaluated with $r_{\max}=r$. Of these, 491 dropped the rod (all with
bounds above 12\,cm), 360 had a zero bound (largest slip 0.49\,mm) and 1{,}309 had a non-zero bound and kept the rod (median
slip/bound 0.023; 7 exceedances, Section~\ref{sec:tight}).
